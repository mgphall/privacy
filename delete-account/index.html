Delete Your Account – All British Hills in the UK (BaggedIt)

This page explains how to delete your account for All British Hills in the UK (BaggedIt), published on Google Play by Matthew Hall, and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the App and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Account & Cloud Backup.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email matthewhall1980@icloud.com from the Google or Apple email address you signed in with, using the subject “Delete my BaggedIt account”. Your account will be deleted and confirmed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (your Google or Apple sign-in link, email address and user ID)
  • Your cloud backup, including hills climbed, adventures, routes, stories and photos
  • Any routes you shared with the community

What is kept

  • The data stored locally on your own phone is not touched — only the cloud copy is removed. You can delete local data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.
  • No other copies of your data are retained after deletion.
