Privacy Policy – Youyou (悠游)

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Youyou (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) plans walks in the city you are visiting and keeps your trip together — walks, tickets and days. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the App, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

In short: your walks, trips, tickets, bucket list and passport are stored on your device and work with the network off. You can use the App without an account. Your documents never leave your phone, not even on a shared trip. There is no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device and, without an account, none of it leaves your device:

  • Walks you have saved, with their stops, descriptions and route
  • Trips, their days, and which walk sits on which day
  • Tickets and documents you attach to a trip (PDFs and images)
  • Places on your bucket list
  • Your passport — the walks you actually finished
  • Progress within a walk: which stops you ticked or skipped
  • Offline map packs you have downloaded
  • Settings: theme, colour and language

Uninstalling the App removes all of it.

2. Tickets and Documents

When you attach a ticket, boarding pass, booking confirmation or insurance certificate to a trip, the file is copied into the App's private storage on your device and stays there. It is never uploaded to us, and it is never sent to the people you share a trip with. That is deliberate: those documents have to work with no signal, which is the point of carrying them.

If you want a copy elsewhere, use your device's own share sheet — the file then goes wherever you send it, under that service's privacy policy rather than ours. Because we hold no copy, losing your device means losing those files, so please keep your own copy of anything important.

Photos and files are chosen through the system picker, so the App only ever receives the specific items you select and has no access to the rest of your library.

3. Location

The App uses your device's location to show your position on the map, to plan a walk around where you are standing, and to notice when you arrive at a stop.

  • This is while-you-use-the-App access only. Youyou does not collect your location in the background and does not track you between sessions.
  • Coordinates are sent to the services in sections 6 and 7 to draw maps, find places, build walking routes and fetch a forecast.
  • Your location is never sold and never used for advertising.

4. Optional Account

You can use Youyou as a guest: saved walks, trips, tickets, the bucket list and your passport all work without an account, offline. Signing in is required for two things only — generating walks with AI, and shared trips.

Sign-in uses Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple through Firebase Authentication. If you sign in, we collect and store:

  • Your email address, and your name if the provider supplies one
  • An account identifier (user ID)
  • How many AI walk generations you have used, and whether you have a subscription
  • The list of shared trips you are a member of

We never see your password. Account data is stored using Google Firebase. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

5. Shared Trips

A trip is private to your device until you create an invite link for it. Once you do, the trip is stored on our servers so the people you invite can see it, and everything on that trip is visible to everyone on it:

  • The trip's name, dates and destination
  • Every walk on the trip, including its stops and route, and who added it
  • The display names of the other members

What is never shared: your tickets and documents (section 2), and your progress within a walk. There are no per-item privacy settings — if you would rather something were not shared, keep it off the shared trip.

The invite link is permanent and cannot be revoked, so please treat it like a key. Removing a member stops them receiving further updates, but anything already saved to their device stays there — we cannot delete content from someone else's phone.

Sharing applies while a trip is being planned. From the first day of the trip the App works from the copy on your device.

6. AI Walk Generation

When you ask Youyou to plan a walk, the request goes to our own server and on to Google's Gemini API. What is sent is the description you typed, the city or area you chose, the date and time of the walk, and the names of places being considered — enough to plan a day and write a line about each stop. Our server does not keep the request after answering it.

Please do not type anything into that box that you would not want processed by Google. Your tickets and documents are never sent to it.

7. Other Services the App Uses

  • Google Places receives search text and coordinates for the area you are planning in, to find real places, their opening hours and photos. Google's privacy policy.
  • Mapbox receives map tile requests, which reveal the area of the map you are viewing. Mapbox's privacy policy.
  • MET Norway receives the coordinates of places on your trip, to return a forecast for each day. Forecast data is licensed CC BY 4.0.
  • Our routing server receives the coordinates you are routing between, to build a walking route that follows real paths. They are not stored or linked to you.
  • Stay22 receives the area of your trip when you open the places-to-stay section, to show nearby accommodation. These are affiliate links and we may earn a commission if you book.

8. What the App Does Not Do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read
  • No analytics and no usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No background location
  • No selling of personal data, and no tracking across other apps or websites

9. Data Retention & Deletion

Data on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

If you created an account, you can permanently delete it from inside the App, under the You tab. Deleting your account erases your sign-in, your account record, and any shared trip you organise — which also stops its invite link working. Walks, trips and documents on your own device are left alone, and you can carry on using Youyou as a guest.

Shared-trip data is kept until the trip's organiser deletes the trip or their account. Nothing you type into AI generation is kept by us after the answer comes back.

10. Your Rights

If you are in the UK or EU you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office. Use the contact address below and we will respond within one month.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It is suitable for general audiences.
